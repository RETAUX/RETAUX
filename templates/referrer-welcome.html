High-converting hooks for your content

⏱ Hook 1 — The procrastination killer

The number one reason people don't claim their tax return is that they think it's complicated. It isn't — and your content can be the thing that finally gets them to act.

The truth: uploading documents to RETAUX takes under 20 minutes. That's it. After that, there's nothing left to do but wait for the money to arrive. No accountant meetings, no confusing ATO portals, no paperwork to print.

And if you don't have formal invoices or receipts? Screenshots of bank transfers, PayID payments, or digital payment confirmations are accepted. Most people have everything they need already on their phone.

Your hook: "I finally did my Australian tax return — it took 18 minutes and I'm getting AUD [X] back. Here's what I uploaded." Show the process. Remove the fear.

⚠️ Hook 2 — The credential warning (this one gets shared)

Many tax agencies ask for your myGov username, password, and SMS verification code. This is dangerous — and if anything goes wrong, the fines fall entirely on the account holder, not on whoever requested the access. The ATO holds the person who granted access liable for any unauthorised activity.

RETAUX never asks for any of that — ever. We work through official ATO authorisation channels with TPB-registered agents only.

Use this in your content: "I didn't know the fine was mine until someone explained it" — it creates urgency, builds trust, and drives DM shares.

🔐 Hook 3 — The ATO lock-out

One of the most searched topics in the Working Holiday community is getting locked out of ATO or myGov. Create content explaining what to do: how to verify identity online, how to link ATO to myGov, what documents they need if the system doesn't recognise them.

Educational content that solves a real frustration builds trust at scale. End the video with: "and once you're in, your tax return is even easier — link in bio."

🔍 Hook 4 — Research your own winning angles

Go to TikTok, search "ATO Australia" or "tax return Australia" and sort by Top. Look at what's already getting millions of views and ask: what question is this answering? That's your hook formula.

The algorithm rewards content that gets sent DM — hooks that make someone think "I need to send this to [name]" outperform polished promotional content every time. Keep it direct, put text on screen, and don't overthink it.
